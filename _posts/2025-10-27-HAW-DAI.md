We are organising the Human-centred AI Workshop (HAW) at the Decentralized AI Conference 2025 in London, on 22 November 2025. Reknowned speakers such as Prof. Weinan Zhang and Prof. Ramesh Raskar will be giving invited talks, together with lots of other interesting talks about making AI more human-centred, in all different aspects.

Date: 22 November 2025
Location: London, UK
