The Solid Symposium 2026 is happening in London, 30 April - 1 May 2026. Proud to be part of the organising team again this year.

Solid Symposium is the international venue for Solid and related technologies for the future of the Internet and data ecosystems. If you are not yet familiar with Solid but interested or worried about the future of the Web and data, this is the right place for you.

Check out further information from the website: https://sosy2026.eu/
Also check out the call for papers and contributions if you want to present or showcase your work or thoughts!

Date: 30 April - 1 May 2026
Location: London, UK
