CSCW'25 was a great event. Apart from talking and discussing with great people about interesting topics, I also presented two of our works, both at the *Responsibly Training Foundation Models* Workshop, and at the main Conference.

The paper talk at the main conference is for *Libertas: Privacy-Preserving Collaborative Computation for Decentralised Personal Data Stores*. It's an user-empowering architecture for enabling loosely cooperating parties to collaboratively contribute data to perform computation **without risking data privacy** (seriouesly, without revelaing their data to anyone), also without losing user autonomy.
